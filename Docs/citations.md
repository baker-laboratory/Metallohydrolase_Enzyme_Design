## Related derivative work: Computational design of metalloproteases

[data and code deposit on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22654831).

## Publication notes

References 1 and 2 appeared online on 3 December 2025; citations and dataset
names use their 2026 issue year. References 3 and 4 appeared online on
15 and 14 October 2026, respectively. As verified on 30 October 2026,
Science has article identifier `eaeh2982` without a volume or issue; the
related derivative Nature paper has no assigned volume, issue, or pages.

Metadata follows publisher-deposited Crossref records
([1](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1038/s41586-025-09746-w),
[2](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1038/s41592-025-02975-x),
[3](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1126/science.aeh2982),
[4](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1038/s41586-026-11167-2)).
[CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff) retains reference 1 as the repository's preferred citation.
